After experiencing the pain points of software testing and QA automation during our work at Quino, we pivoted to focus on solving this burning issue. The platform leverages artificial intelligence to automatically generate, execute, and maintain comprehensive test suites, significantly reducing testing time while improving coverage and reliability for development teams.
