Bacprep.ro is an AI-based learning platform specifically designed to help high school students from the Hungarian minority in Romania prepare for their final exams. As Product Designer, Tech Lead, and AI Developer, I led the development of the MVP in just 3 weeks. The platform combines intelligent content delivery with personalized learning paths, making exam preparation more efficient and accessible for students navigating the unique challenges of minority education in Romania.
