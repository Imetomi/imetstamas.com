Quino is an AI-based learning platform I co-founded and led as CEO, targeted at students and higher education. We raised an $840k pre-seed round and scaled to over 100,000 users. The platform implements advanced RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) techniques to help students learn more effectively. What started as a simple learning assist tool I created in 12th grade evolved into a full-fledged edtech startup that revolutionizes how students interact with educational content.
